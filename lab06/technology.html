Deep Tech Architecture

We build resilient, user-centric core systems. Our technology stack merges advanced AI inferences, deterministic embedded hardware, and decentralized networks into a seamless, high-performance ecosystem.

Hardware & Embedded

At the physical layer lies the ESP32 microcontroller running MicroPython. Precision data acquisition is achieved through calibrated DHT11 and TDS sensors, stabilized by the L7805CV voltage regulator.

AI & Computer Vision

Our diagnostic engine utilizes the state-of-the-art YOLOv8 model. Optimized for edge inference, it performs real-time visual analysis of plant pathology, instantly identifying diseases and pest infestations with unprecedented accuracy.

Decentralized Network

Ensuring data immutability and automated governance, our architecture integrates seamlessly with the SUI Ecosystem. We leverage the Move Language to deploy secure smart contracts, governing device clusters on a truly decentralized infrastructure.
